## Design review: Transformadora de Hules y Plásticos (`thp-web` — `redesign-2026`)

### Summary
Overall rating: **Excellent**.
This B2B industrial catalog and direct-plant quoting site for Transformadora de Hules y Plásticos (Zapopan, Jalisco) has a single clear job: let footwear manufacturers and industrial buyers inspect THP's five crepe/SBR rubber sheet references (`CLA0CR`, `CRA0CR`, `CRA0ZG`, `CRN0CR`, `CRN0ZG`), molded outsoles, and gauges (`fierrajes 5, 6, 9, 12`) and request a direct factory quote without middlemen. The design will be remembered by its warm unbleached-paper ground (`#FAF9F6` / `#F0EEE9`) paired with THP's industrial crimson press mark (`#9F013B`) and darkroom-style `picture-develops` transitions (`film` look) that frame macro rubber textures like physical plant specimen cards.

**Scope & artifacts reviewed:** Web application (`TanStack Start` + `Solid 2.0` + `@ui-sdk/solid`) audited against Apple's eight design principles (`design-principles.md`), foundations (`accessibility.md`, `color.md`, `typography.md`, `layout.md`, `writing.md`, `motion.md`, `branding.md`), and component guidelines (`buttons.md`, `entering-data.md`, `toggles.md`, `lists-and-tables.md`) across Lenses 1, 3, 4, and 5, using computed CSS/token values and full-page screenshots at `1440×900` and `390×844` (`shots/final/{home,catalogo,sku-cla0cr,contacto}-{1440,390}.png`).

- **Resolved before final shoot (High — Mobile Product Context Photo Upscaling):** Post-merge CSS rule ordering in `src/styles.css` caused `.develop` (`height: min(500px, var(--ly-fit))`) to override `.sku-context-photo` at `390px`, stretching the `480×360` image `1.39×` vertically (`layout.md › Adaptability`: *"don't change the aspect ratio of the artwork; instead, scale it so that important visual content remains visible"*). ### Craft notes
- **Point of view & restraint (`design-principles.md › Craft`, `branding.md › Best practices`):** Rather than defaulting to generic dark-mode SaaS clichés or decorative 3D renders, the interface treats rubber compounding as a tactile material catalog. The warm paper ground (`#FAF9F6`) lets amber crepe (`#FFBF00`), dark crepe (`#5A5A5A`), and carbon-black zig-zag ridges read with true colour fidelity, while the crimson brand ground (`#9F013B`) is spent once per narrative flow (`#planta` on `/`, `#grabados` on `/laminas`, `#matriceria` on `/suelas`).
- **Typography (`typography.md › Ensuring legibility`, `typography.md › Conveying hierarchy`):** Self-hosted variable `Albert Sans` (`400` display weight with `-0.035em` / `-0.025em` optical tracking; `400` body at `16px–20px`; `500` labels and subheads; `tabular-nums` on all gauges and phone numbers) establishes a crisp hierarchy without heavy bold weights (`>500` avoided on display headings).
- **Motion (`motion.md › Best practices`, `accessibility.md › Cognitive`):** Two restrained moments — `word-highlight` on `#proceso` and `picture-develops` (`film` look) on plant/product frames — reinforce the manufacturing narrative and disable cleanly under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` in `src/styles.css`.

### What works
- **AAA/AA Contrast Across Every Surface (`accessibility.md › Vision`, `color.md › Inclusive color`):** Primary ink `#0C1221` on `#FAF9F6` achieves **`17.74:1`** (`16.11:1` on `#F0EEE9`); secondary charcoal `#363636` achieves **`11.48:1`** (`10.42:1` on `#F0EEE9`); crimson action `#9F013B` with `#FAF9F6` text achieves **`7.85:1`**; dark footer `#0C1221` with `#FAF9F6` text achieves **`17.74:1`** and `#95989E` muted text achieves **`6.46:1`**. Logo metallic gray (`#8E959B`, `2.88:1` on light ground) is strictly confined to the graphic mark and never used for text or borders.
- **Color-Blind Safe Filter & Swatch System (`color.md › Inclusive color`, `toggles.md › Best practices`):** Every colour filter pill and SKU swatch pairs its `.swatch-dot` (`aria-hidden="true"`) with an explicit Spanish label (`Ámbar`, `Oscura`, `Rojiza`, `Blanco`, `Café`, `Negro`), a `45%` ink border on `#FFFFFF`, and `aria-pressed` / `data-pressed` state via `@ui-sdk/solid/toggle` (`ToggleGroup` / `Toggle`) plus an `aria-live="polite"` result counter.
- **One-Screen Section Discipline & Pre-Filled Quoting Flow (`layout.md › Visual hierarchy`, `entering-data.md › Best practices`, `lists-and-tables.md › Content`):** Every section fits its `100svh` (`--h: 1` or `--h: 2`) budget across `1440×900`, `1024×768`, `820×1180`, `768×1024`, and `390×844` with zero horizontal bleed or clipping, and every SKU card and comparison row links directly to `/catalogo/$sku` or pre-fills `/contacto?sku=<SKU>` so buyers never re-type the reference code.
